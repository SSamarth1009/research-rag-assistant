OVERVIEW
--------
Nvidia Corporation is an American multinational technology company headquartered in
Santa Clara, California. The company develops graphics processing units (GPUs),
systems on chips (SoCs), and application programming interfaces (APIs) for data
science, high-performance computing, video games, and mobile and automotive
applications. Founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem,
Nvidia has been widely described as a Big Tech company and a dominant force in the
AI hardware market.

HISTORY
-------
Late 1992  Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem met at a Denny's diner
           on Berryessa Road in East San Jose and agreed to start a company focused
           on graphics-based computing.

1993       Nvidia incorporated on April 5, 1993. The three founders initially worked
           in Priem's townhouse in Fremont, California. Started with $40,000 in the
           bank. Received $20 million in venture capital funding from Sequoia Capital,
           Sutter Hill Ventures, and others. Name derived from "invidia" (Latin for
           "envy"), chosen after many rejected candidates including "NVision".

1994–1999  During the late 1990s, Nvidia was one of ~70 startups pursuing graphics
           acceleration. Only two survived: Nvidia and ATI Technologies (later
           acquired by AMD).
           First product: NV1 graphics accelerator (used quadrilateral primitives),
           which failed commercially after Microsoft's DirectX standardized on
           triangles instead.

Early 2000s  Nvidia invested over $1 billion to develop CUDA, a parallel computing
             platform allowing GPUs to run massively parallel programs for a wide
             range of compute-intensive applications.

1999       Initial public offering (IPO) on Nasdaq.

2020       Mellanox Technologies acquired, strengthening Nvidia's data center and
           networking capabilities.

2023       Nvidia became the seventh U.S. company to reach a $1 trillion market
           valuation, driven by soaring demand for AI hardware.

2025       Nvidia became the first company in the world to surpass $4 trillion and
           subsequently $5 trillion in market capitalization, driven by demand for
           AI data center hardware during the AI boom. Holds over 80% of the market
           for GPUs used in training and deploying AI models. Powers over 75% of
           TOP500 supercomputers worldwide.

AI & COMPUTING SIGNIFICANCE
----------------------------
OpenAI developed ChatGPT (launched 2022) using a supercomputer powered by
10,000 Nvidia GPUs. Nvidia's GPU technology excels at parallel processing —
using many cores to allow multiple processes to run simultaneously — which
proved ideally suited for training large AI models.
