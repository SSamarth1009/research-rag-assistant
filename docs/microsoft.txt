OVERVIEW
--------
Microsoft Corporation is an American multinational technology company headquartered
in Redmond, Washington. It became influential in the rise of personal computers
through software like Windows and has since expanded into areas such as Internet
services, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, video gaming, and more. A Big
Tech company, Microsoft is the largest software company by revenue, one of the most
valuable public companies, and one of the most valuable brands globally.

HISTORY
-------
1972    Bill Gates and Paul Allen founded Traf-O-Data to analyze automobile traffic data.
1975    Microsoft established on April 4, 1975 in Albuquerque, New Mexico, after Gates
        and Allen developed a BASIC interpreter for the Altair 8800 microcomputer.
        Allen suggested the name "Micro-Soft" (short for micro-computer software).
1977    First international office opened in Japan (ASCII Microsoft).
1979    Headquarters moved to Bellevue, Washington.
1980    IBM awarded Microsoft a contract to provide the OS for the IBM PC. Microsoft
        purchased 86-DOS and rebranded it as MS-DOS.
1981    IBM PC released with MS-DOS. Microsoft retained OS ownership.
1983    Microsoft Mouse released; Microsoft Press publishing division launched.
        Paul Allen resigned after being diagnosed with Hodgkin's lymphoma.
1985    Windows 1.0 released (November 20) — a graphical extension for MS-DOS.
1986    Headquarters moved to Redmond, Washington. IPO on NASDAQ (March 13).
        The IPO created 3 billionaires and ~12,000 millionaires among employees.
1990    Windows 3.0 released — became widely popular (60 million copies sold).
        Federal Trade Commission began examining Microsoft for potential collusion.
1993    Windows NT released.
2000    Steve Ballmer replaced Gates as CEO.
2002    Microsoft launched the .NET initiative and new developer tools.
2004    Windows XP Media Center Edition 2005 released.
2009    Windows 7 released, praised by reviewers.
2011    Skype acquired for $8.5 billion (then-largest acquisition).
2012    Windows 8 released; Surface line of hybrid tablets introduced.
2014    Satya Nadella became CEO, refocusing the company on cloud computing.
2016    LinkedIn acquired for $26.2 billion.
2018    Joined the Open Invention Network community (holding 60,000+ patents).
        Won contract to supply 100,000 HoloLens headsets to the U.S. military.
2019    Became third U.S. publicly traded company valued over $1 trillion (April).
2022    Microsoft Gaming division established.
2023    Activision Blizzard acquired for $68.7 billion — largest-ever acquisition.
2025    Cloud revenue represents ~61% of total company revenue.
