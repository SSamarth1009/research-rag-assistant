OVERVIEW
--------
Tesla, Inc. is an American multinational automotive and clean energy company
headquartered in Austin, Texas. It designs, manufactures, and sells battery electric
vehicles (BEVs), stationary battery energy storage devices (from home to grid-scale),
solar panels and solar shingles, and related products and services. The company is
named in tribute to inventor and electrical engineer Nikola Tesla. Tesla is one of
the world's most valuable companies by market capitalization and was the world's most
valuable automaker starting in July 2020.

HISTORY
-------
July 2003    Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning incorporated Tesla Motors, Inc.
             in San Carlos, California. Eberhard served as CEO, Tarpenning as CFO.
             Goal: build "a car manufacturer that is also a technology company,"
             with core technologies in battery, software, and motors.

February 2004  Elon Musk led Tesla's Series A funding round of $7.5 million,
               contributing $6.5 million himself (from his $176 million PayPal exit).
               Musk became Chairman and largest shareholder.

May 2004     J.B. Straubel joined as Chief Technical Officer.

2005         Series B funding ($13 million), adding Valor Equity Partners.

May 2006     $40 million Series C round; investors included Google co-founders
             Sergey Brin and Larry Page, and ex-eBay President Jeff Skoll.

May 2007     Series D: $45 million. Total private financing exceeded $105 million.

August 2007  Eberhard asked to step down as CEO by the board (led by Musk).
             Michael Marks became interim CEO; Ze'ev Drori became CEO in December.

January 2008  Eberhard and Tarpenning both left the company.

2008         Elon Musk succeeded Drori as CEO (October 2008).
             Tesla began Roadster production in Menlo Park (a former Chevrolet
             dealership's service bays). By January 2009: $187 million raised
             and 147 cars delivered.

2009         Lawsuit settlement allowed all five (Eberhard, Tarpenning, Wright,
             Musk, Straubel) to be recognized as co-founders.

2010         Tesla went public on Nasdaq (IPO).

2012         Model S began production — a full-sized electric sedan.

2015         Model X SUV launched.

2017         Model 3 launched — Tesla's first mass-market vehicle.

2020         Model Y crossover launched. Tesla became the world's most valuable
             automaker by market capitalization (July).

Oct 2021 –   Tesla became a US$1 trillion company (6th U.S. company to do so).
Mar 2022

2022         Tesla Semi truck began production.

2023         Cybertruck launched. Tesla ranked 69th in Forbes Global 2000.
             Tesla led the BEV market with 17.6% share in 2024.

Nov 2024     Tesla exceeded $1 trillion market cap again.

Nov 2025     Tesla board approved a $1 trillion pay package for Elon Musk
             (performance-based, over 10 years).

End of 2025  Tesla lost its status as world's leading electric vehicle manufacturer
             by unit volume.

CONTROVERSIES & CRITICISMS
---------------------------
Tesla has been subject to lawsuits, boycotts, and government scrutiny, including:
  - Allegations of whistleblower retaliation
  - Worker rights violations (sexual harassment, anti-union activities)
  - Safety defects leading to dozens of vehicle recalls
  - Overpromising on Full Self-Driving (FSD) capabilities and timelines
  - Controversial public statements by CEO Elon Musk

NOTABLE FACTS
