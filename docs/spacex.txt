OVERVIEW
--------
Space Exploration Technologies Corporation, doing business as SpaceX, is a private
American aerospace and artificial intelligence company headquartered at the Starbase
development site in Starbase, Texas. Since its founding in 2002, SpaceX has made
numerous advances in rocket propulsion, reusable launch vehicles, human spaceflight,
and satellite constellation technology. As of 2026, SpaceX conducts more orbital
launches annually than any other launch provider, including private competitors and
national programs such as China's.

KEY PRODUCTS & LAUNCH VEHICLES
--------------------------------
Falcon 1:         First privately developed liquid-fueled rocket to reach orbit.
                  Flew 5 times (2006–2009) from Omelek Island.
Falcon 9:         Medium-lift, two-stage orbital rocket. As of May 2025, the
                  first stage has landed and re-flown 450+ times (1–3 launches/week).
Falcon Heavy:     Heavy-lift rocket built from three Falcon 9 cores. First flew 2018.
Dragon 1:         Cargo capsule for ISS resupply (COTS missions for NASA).
Dragon 2:         Crew capsule; began crewed NASA missions in 2020.
Starship:         Super heavy-lift, fully reusable launch system — the largest
                  launch vehicle in history. Under active development and testing.
Starlink:         Satellite internet constellation (operational since 2019);
                  primary revenue generator for SpaceX.
Starshield:       Military satellite communications counterpart to Starlink.
Space Suits:      Developed for human spaceflight via the Polaris Program.

HISTORY
-------
Early 2001  Elon Musk met Robert Zubrin (Mars Society) and donated $100,000.
            Announced "Mars Oasis" — a project to land a greenhouse on Mars.

2001–2002   Musk attempted to purchase a Dnepr rocket from Russia for the project
            but was rebuffed. On the flight home, he decided to start his own
            rocket company instead, applying vertical integration and commercial
            off-the-shelf components to cut launch costs.

March 2002  SpaceX founded in El Segundo, California. Musk personally interviewed
            early employees including rocket engineer Tom Mueller (CTO), Gwynne
            Shotwell (COO), and Chris Thompson (VP of Operations), who came from
            TRW and Boeing. Gwynne Shotwell hired as head of sales in August 2002.

2005        SpaceX had 160 employees by November.

2006–2008   Falcon 1 first launches. First three attempts failed.
2008        Falcon 1 achieved successful orbital launch (4th attempt).
            SpaceX secured NASA Commercial Orbital Transportation Services (COTS)
            contracts for ISS deliveries using Dragon capsule.

2010        Falcon 9 first flight.

2012        SpaceX completed all COTS test flights and began operational Commercial
            Resupply Services missions to the ISS. Began developing Falcon 9
            first-stage reusability hardware.

2015        First successful Falcon 9 first-stage landing (December).

2017        First re-launch of a recovered Falcon 9 first stage (SES-10 mission).

2018        Falcon Heavy first flew (February), after more than a decade in development.

2019        First operational Starlink satellite went online.

2020        Dragon 2 began crewed NASA missions. Starship test prototypes begin
            construction and testing.

2022        Starshield (military satellite service) began development.

2025        Falcon 9 first stage has flown again 450+ times. SpaceX is developing
            the Starship Human Landing System (HLS) under NASA's Artemis program
            for lunar missions. SpaceX expected to pursue an IPO in 2026.

LAUNCH CAPABILITIES
-------------------
SpaceX conducts more orbital launches annually than any other provider worldwide
(private or government-affiliated). Falcon 9 operates at 1–3 launches per week.
Launch facilities include:
  - SLC-40, Cape Canaveral Space Force Station, Florida
  - LC-39A, Kennedy Space Center, Florida
  - SLC-4E, Vandenberg Space Force Base, California
  - Starbase, Boca Chica, Texas (Starship development and launch site)

MISSION & VISION
----------------
Elon Musk founded SpaceX with the goal of reducing the cost of access to space
by a factor of ten and enabling the long-term colonization of Mars. The company
pursues fully reusable launch vehicles as the primary means to achieve this.
SpaceX also works closely with NASA and the U.S. Armed Forces through government
contracts. In 2026, SpaceX deprioritized Mars ambitions to focus on near-term
operational programs.
