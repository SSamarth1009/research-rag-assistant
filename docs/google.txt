OVERVIEW
--------
Google LLC is an American multinational technology corporation focused on information
technology, online advertising, search engine technology, cloud computing, software,
quantum computing, e-commerce, consumer electronics, and artificial intelligence (AI).
It has been referred to as "the most powerful company in the world" by the BBC, and
is one of the world's most valuable brands. Google is a wholly owned subsidiary of
Alphabet Inc., its parent holding company.

HISTORY
-------
January 1996  Larry Page and Sergey Brin began a research project at Stanford
              University, studying the mathematical link structure of the Web.
              Their algorithm PageRank determined a page's relevance by the
              number and importance of pages linking back to it. Unofficial
              "third founder" Scott Hassan wrote much of the original code.

1997          The search engine was renamed "Google" — a deliberate misspelling
              of "googol" (10^100), signifying the vast quantity of information
              it aimed to index.

August 1998   Google received its first investment: $100,000 from Andy
              Bechtolsheim, co-founder of Sun Microsystems.

September 4, 1998  Google officially incorporated in Menlo Park, California.

June 1999     New funding round of $25 million, with major investors Kleiner
              Perkins and Sequoia Capital.

2000          Google began selling keyword-based advertisements (text ads to
              maintain page speed and design). This laid the foundation for
              Google's dominant advertising business model.

2003          Google acquired Pyra Labs (Blogger). Moved to Googleplex in
              Mountain View (leased from Silicon Graphics at 1600 Amphitheatre
              Parkway; purchased in 2006 for $319 million).

2004          IPO (initial public offering). Google News launched (2002),
              Gmail launched.

2005          Google Maps launched.

2006          YouTube acquired for $1.65 billion.

2008          Google Chrome browser launched.

2014          Google acquired DeepMind Technologies (AI company) for ~$400 million.

2015          Google reorganized as a wholly owned subsidiary of the newly
              formed Alphabet Inc. (October 2, 2015). Sundar Pichai became
              CEO of Google; Larry Page became CEO of Alphabet.

2019          Google+ social network shut down (April). Larry Page and Sergey
              Brin resigned from executive roles at Alphabet (December).

2019          Sundar Pichai became CEO of Alphabet Inc. (December 3, 2019).

2022          Stock split completed (mid-year). Google laid off ~12,000
              employees (6% of global workforce) in January 2023.

2025          Alphabet acquired Wiz (cloud security company) for $32 billion —
              its largest-ever acquisition.

PARENT COMPANY — ALPHABET INC.
--------------------------------
Alphabet Inc. was created on October 2, 2015, through a restructuring of Google.
It became the parent holding company of Google and several former Google subsidiaries,
including X Development, Calico, Nest, Verily, Fiber, CapitalG, and GV. Alphabet is
listed as the world's second-largest technology company by revenue (after Nvidia),
the largest technology company by profit, and among the world's most valuable companies.

MARKET LEADERSHIP
-----------------
Google is the world's largest provider of:
  - Search engines
  - Mapping and navigation applications
  - Email services
  - Office suites
  - Online video platforms
  - Photo and cloud storage
  - Mobile operating systems (Android)
  - Web browsers (Chrome)
  - Machine learning frameworks (TensorFlow)
  - AI virtual assistants (Google Assistant / Gemini)

Google Search and YouTube are the two most-visited websites in the world.
Google was ranked 2nd most valuable brand by Forbes (January 2022) and
4th by Interbrand (February 2022).

NOTABLE FACTS
-------------
- In 1998, Page and Brin proposed to sell Google to Yahoo for $1 million — refused.
- Larry Page co-founded Alphabet after the restructuring of Google in 2015.
- Has faced criticism over privacy, tax avoidance, censorship, antitrust issues,
  and abuse of monopoly position.
- Google acquired on average more than one company per week in 2010 and 2011.
- As of 2025, Alphabet has acquired over 200 companies in total.
